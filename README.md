# Chuang Xiao

**Geophysics Ph.D. candidate at Colorado School of Mines**, specializing in **distributed fiber-optic sensing (DFOS)** for production, reservoir, and seismic monitoring.

My research combines acoustic measurements, signal processing, and machine learning to understand subsurface processes.

### Research Projects

- **Multiphase-Flow Monitoring**: Using distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) to characterize oil–water flow, investigate flow-induced vibration, and develop physics-informed and data-driven models for flow-rate estimation.

- **Poseidon Project — Seabed DAS**: Evaluating seabed fiber response for offshore reservoir and carbon storage monitoring in the Norwegian North Sea, with a focus on seismic amplitudes, fiber coupling, and shallow sediment properties.

- **Machine Learning for Microseismic Analysis**: Developed interactive DAS phase-picking tools and trained a PyTorch-based PhaseNet model, with applications to microseismic monitoring at Utah FORGE.
